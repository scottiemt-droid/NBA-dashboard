Method

  • Projection = the board's own projection (minutes first, attempts × efficiency, pace), blending vs-team 30 / own form 40 / opponent positional (full season) 30, read from the board code, not recomputed.
  • Probability: every game in his current-role vs-team meetings and his last 10 → per-36 × projected minutes, weighted by the blend weights split across each line's games, rescaled so the mean equals the projection; P(stat ≥ rung) from a Gaussian-kernel smooth of that weighted distribution (continuity-corrected, clamped 1–99%). Small samples make these probabilities noisy — check n on the board card.
  • Devig: two-way (over + under at the same book and line) → proportional. One-sided alt rungs → that book's own main-line margin for the player/stat, applied proportionally. A rung that can't be devigged doesn't count toward the bar.
  • Edge = model probability − devigged implied probability. A leg qualifies when ≥ 3 books each show an edge ≥ 3%. "Edge 3rd book" is the edge at the third-best book — the honest strength of the 3-book bar.
  • Not headlined: legs that clear only with vacated minutes / usage bump added (base projection fails the bar), or that the board flags with a minutes-scaling caution.
  • Stake: ¼ Kelly at the best verified price, capped 1% per single and 0.5% per parlay, as % of bankroll. Never a dollar amount.
  • The agent never places bets. These are matchup-based opportunities to review, not locks.
