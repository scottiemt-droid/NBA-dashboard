How this board is built (sources, labels, what is modeled)
  • Sources: ESPN public endpoints — completed box scores (this season), player game logs (prior two seasons, for vs-team meetings only), slate, market line, rosters and injury statuses. Nothing is typed in by hand.
  • Projection = weighted blend of three per-36 lines × projected minutes. Default weights 50% vs-team / 25% own last 5 / 25% opponent positional log, adjustable above. A line with n=0 is dropped and the others renormalized (shown on the card). No last-season baseline.
  • Positions: ESPN lists players only as G / F / C. The PG/SG and SF/PF split is inferred from each player's box profile (G: AST/36 ≥ 4.5 → PG else SG; F: REB/36 ≥ 10.5 → C, ≥ 7 → PF, else SF). Every position-bucketed number therefore carries a MODELED BUCKET label — the stats are box-score sums, the bucket assignment is a model.
  • Opponent positional log: in each of the opponent's last 5 games, the opposing player at that position who played the most minutes. Raw lines are shown game by game, with per-36 for the blend.
  • Vs-team line: his last 5 meetings with tonight's opponent across this season and the two prior seasons (incl. playoffs, incl. prior clubs). Meetings with a prior club or in a role ≥8 mpg different are split out and not blended.
  • Modeled (labelled where shown): projected score (pace × ORtg/DRtg from box scores, each regressed toward league average by GP/(GP+10) so 7-game samples don't swing it, ±1.2 home court per side, no injury adjustment); vacated minutes allocation (same position → adjacent → any rotation player, +8 min / 38 min caps, remainder left unallocated); usage bump (50% of the vacated production not recovered by minutes); blowout minutes trim on projected starters (from |spread| ≥ 10).
  • Not available from any box-score source, so not shown: defensive assignments and coverage scheme (drop / switch / zone). Read those yourself.
  • Scheme tags are team tendencies read off the team-vs-position panel, not defensive assignments or coverage schemes. PERIMETER-WEAK: PG+SG+SF points and 3PM allowed both ≥ 8% above the league average for those buckets. INTERIOR-WEAK: PF+C points and rebounds allowed both ≥ 8% above the league average for those buckets. A team can carry both or neither; every team panel shows the numbers whether or not a tag fired.
  • Screen flags are matchup signals, not picks, in two tiers. MATCHUP FLAG (tonight, no history needed): his own last 5 per-36 is at or past his season per-36 rate, his current-role vs-team per-36 (blowout meetings dropped when others remain) is ≥ 5% past it the same way, and the opponent's positional log is ≥ 5% past its players' own norms the same way, still true without its single most extreme game. On top of that, the blended per-36 must be ≥ 15% past his season rate, and the gap at projected minutes must be at least 2.5 PTS / 1.2 REB / 1.0 AST / 0.5 3PM / 0.4 STL or BLK. PATTERN badge: the gap also repeated in ≥3 separate games and most of the sample (unchanged rule). A pattern without tonight's three-line agreement still shows, labelled as such. The positional-log screen reads each opposing player against his own season rate (excluding that game), so "a starter scored more than this bench player" doesn't count as an edge. The vs-team screen compares each meeting to his current per-36 rate. STL/BLK flags only show alongside a PTS/REB/AST/3PM flag, unless a vs-team STL/BLK line is consistently 2+ with positional-log backing.
  • Refresh: the board rebuilds every time it opens and again at 8:07 AM ET if left open. Box scores and prior-season logs are cached in this browser, so later loads are fast.
